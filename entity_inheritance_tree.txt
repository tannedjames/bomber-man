Bomberman Game Entity Inheritance Tree
=====================================

Base Entity Types (from FXGL)
----------------------------
Entity (com.almasb.fxgl.entity.Entity)
    ├── Component (com.almasb.fxgl.entity.component.Component)
    │   ├── PhysicsComponent
    │   ├── CollidableComponent
    │   └── IrremovableComponent

Game Entities
------------
1. Static Entities (Environment)
   ├── Wall
   ├── Brick
   │   └── BrickBreak
   ├── Door
   └── Background

2. Dynamic Entities
   ├── Player
   │   └── PlayerComponent
   │       └── PlayerSkin (NORMAL, GOLD)
   │
   ├── Enemy (Abstract)
   │   ├── Enemy1
   │   ├── Enemy2 (Chases player when close)
   │   ├── Enemy3 (Splits into Enemy1 when hit)
   │   ├── Enemy4
   │   └── Enemy5 (Fastest chaser)
   │
   ├── Bomb
   │   ├── VirtualBomb
   │   └── BombComponent
   │
   ├── Flame
   │   ├── CentralFlame
   │   ├── TopDownFlame
   │   ├── TopUpFlame
   │   ├── TopRightFlame
   │   ├── TopLeftFlame
   │   ├── UpFlame
   │   ├── DownFlame
   │   ├── LeftFlame
   │   ├── RightFlame
   │   └── FlameComponent
   │
   └── Powerups
       ├── PowerupFlames
       ├── PowerupBombs
       ├── PowerupSpeed
       ├── PowerupFlamepass
       └── PowerupLife

State System
-----------
DynamicEntityState.State
├── UP
├── RIGHT
├── DOWN
├── LEFT
├── STOP
└── DIE

Notes:
- All entities inherit from FXGL's base Entity class
- Components are used to add specific behaviors to entities
- Enemies have different behaviors and movement patterns
- Flame entities represent different parts of bomb explosions
- Powerups provide various player enhancements
- The state system manages entity movement and status
